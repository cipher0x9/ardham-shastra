Dated, because the campus is dated

A timeline of the ladder

This is not a substitute journey. The campus names eras, so this page lines those eras up. The spine is Module 27. Modules 02, 06, 10, and 18, plus the citation file and the FAQ, supply the other dates. Where two modules phrase the same life differently, both phrases are kept.

The practice order is on the study path, not here. Piṅgala sits at about 300 BCE beside Kaṇāda even though the idea-ladder table prints Piṅgala after the Sāṃkhya rung.

  1. about 1500 BCE

    Vedas

    Oral transmission as technology. Sound is the vehicle of knowledge.

    Module 27

  2. about 600 BCE

    Upaniṣads

    The campus gloss is listen long · speak true: observer and observed are one.

    Module 27 · Module 10

  3. about 500 BCE

    Pāṇini

    Aṣṭādhyāyī, called the first generative grammar on the idea-ladder. Module 03 also dates that text to about the 4th century BCE.

    Module 27 · Module 03

  4. about 400 BCE

    Gautama

    Nyāya: logic as a science, and the five-member syllogism.

    Module 27 · Module 04

  5. about 300 BCE

    Kaṇāda

    Vaiśeṣika: categories of what exists.

    Module 27

  6. about 300 BCE

    Piṅgala

    Binary enumeration and the mātrā-meru recurrence. Module 06 places the Chandaḥśāstra in about the 3rd century BCE.

    Module 27 · Module 06

  7. about 200 BCE

    Īśvarakṛṣṇa

    Sāṃkhya and the 25-tattva layer stack.

    Module 27

  8. 476 CE

    Āryabhaṭa

    π ≈ 3.1416, eclipses explained, reflected light. The campus line: models beat myth.

    Module 06 · Module 10 · Module 27

  9. about 500 CE

    Sūrya Siddhānta

    Planetary computation. Module 10 adds the mahāyuga of 4.32 million years: the sky is computable and time is vast.

    Module 27 · Module 10

  10. 1114–1185

    Bhāskara II

    Gravity intuition, calculus seeds, and what the ladder calls zero mastery.

    Module 06 · Module 27

  11. 1202

    Fibonacci’s publication

    Module 06 notes that the mātrā-meru count is the sequence Fibonacci published in 1202.

    Module 06

  12. about 1400

    Mādhava

    Infinite series for π. Module 18 writes π/4 = 1 − 1/3 + 1/5 − 1/7 + … and names Mādhava of Sangamagrama.

    Module 27 · Module 18

  13. about 1530

    Jyeṣṭhadeva

    Yuktibhāṣā: proofs, not only results. Series for π, sine, and cosine.

    Module 06 · Module 27

  14. 1637–1687

    Descartes to Newton

    The European rung: method, calculus, universal gravitation. Module 10 names Copernicus, Kepler, and Newton without years: one law, everywhere.

    Module 27 · Module 10

  15. 1887–1920

    Rāmānujan

    Pattern intuition at the frontier, with proof later.

    Module 06 · Module 27

  16. 1929

    Hubble

    An expanding universe. The campus line: space itself grows.

    Module 10

  17. 1945 and after

    Computation

    Turing, von Neumann, and Chomsky: computation, information, and formal grammars.

    Module 27

  18. 2006

    Spacing effect

    Cepeda and colleagues: distributed practice beat massed practice across about 250 experiments.

    Module 02

  19. 2007

    Interleaving

    Rohrer and Taylor: mixing problem types beat blocked practice in math and category learning.

    Module 02

  20. 2011

    Testing and desirable difficulty

    Roediger and Butler on the testing effect. Bjork and Bjork: harder encoding improves long-term retention.

    Module 02

  21. 2013

    Sleep consolidation

    Rasch and Born: memory reactivates in slow-wave sleep; sleep after learning boosts recall.

    Module 02

  22. 2015 and after

    Spacetime, measured

    Gravitational waves, and the Event Horizon image. The campus line: spacetime is real and bendable.

    Module 10

  23. 2016

    Deliberate practice

    Ericsson and Pool. The campus calls the round “10,000 hours” a myth; feedback is what matters.

    Module 02

  24. 2017 and after

    Learned grammars at scale

    The ladder’s latest rung: transformers and large language models, described as Pāṇini’s dream automated.

    Module 27

  25. the 2020s

    JWST

    Module 10 pairs the cosmic microwave background and deep-field images with JWST: baby pictures of the universe.

    Module 10

  26. 2026

    The campus present

    Modules on assisted learning, the engineering stack, and the toolbox are written as a 2026 map. The method underneath is the same seven steps.
